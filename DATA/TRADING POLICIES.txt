# Candle Brain — Active Entry Policies (PDF merge)

Pipeline: Detect → Bible (RAM) → ML cost-aware → Fire

## Active pattern families (Bible priority — fire allowlist)
1. Engulfing / Piercing / Dark Cloud
2. Pin bar / Hammer / Shooting Star
3. Morning / Evening Star
4. Inside-bar break (Bible)

(Disabled for fire: harami, tweezers, doji extremes, three white/black, belt, marubozu)

## Filters
- EMA50 / EMA200 trend + local slope
- Engulfing: full body cover (Nison) + with-trend preferred; counter-trend only on exhaustion
- No impulse chase: block when stretched far above/below EMA8+EMA21
- Max 1 same-side auto position per pair
- Min 3 bars between auto entries
- **3-candle entry:** bar1 pattern → bar2 direction confirm → fire at bar3 open
- Volume ≥ 1.6× Vol MA and expanding vs previous (≥1.15×)
- Min pattern strength ≥ 0.75
- Same-bar bull+bear conflict = NO_TRADE
- ML cost-aware: λ=1.2 × fee hurdle + abs candle range ≥ 0.02%

## Exits
Profit lock +0.40% floor / 1.5× trail from peak / hard +1.2%.
Stop-loss (reverse): arm at −0.40% / 1.5× trail from trough / hard −1.2%.
