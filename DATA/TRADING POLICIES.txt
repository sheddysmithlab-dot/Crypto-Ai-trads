# Candle Brain — Active Entry / Exit Policies

## Timeframe routing
| TF | Engine | File |
|----|--------|------|
| **1M** | Fade opposite (Doji / Engulfing) | `backend/1min.py` |
| **5M+** | Fire Engine v3.1 | `backend/fire_trade_engine.py` |

---

## 1M engine — ENTRY
1. Closed 1m candle: detect **Doji** or **Bullish/Bearish Engulfing**
2. Trade the **OPPOSITE** side (fade)
3. Max **1 new trade per minute** (global)
4. Hold up to **10** open trades
5. No pattern that minute → skip

## 1M engine — EXIT (batch only)
- **No** per-trade SL/TP
- **No** trailing profit-book / structure SL / TF hard-stop
- When **10** trades are open AND combined **net P&L after broker fees ≥ +2% of batch capital** → **close all 10**
- Reset batch baseline → open next 10 → repeat
- Manual close + emergency sell-all still available

Env: `MIN1_MAX_OPEN`, `MIN1_BATCH_PROFIT_PCT`, `MIN1_SIZE_FRAC`

---

## Fire Engine (5M+) — ENTRY
Patterns + shadow psych + market structure + EMA/MACD/ADX/RSI confluence

## Fire Engine (5M+) — EXIT
- SL = pattern extreme ± ATR pad
- TP = 1:2 R:R
- Auto-exit when mark hits SL or TP
- Manual close + emergency sell-all still available
